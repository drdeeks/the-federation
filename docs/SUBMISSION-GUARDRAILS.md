# Submission and Attribution Guardrails

These rules protect the owner and the Federation project from accidental or
invented attribution.

## Authorship

- Do not add people, collaborators, companies, agencies, sponsors, or
  organizations as authors, teammates, or co-builders unless the owner
  explicitly names and authorizes them.
- Do not claim that an AI system, coding agent, model, or assistant authored,
  co-authored, submitted, or owns The Federation.
- Do not invent a team, company, repository, demo, deployment, result, or
  affiliation.
- The Federation is the owner's project. External tools and systems are
  implementation dependencies or integrations, not authors of Federation.

## Technical disclosure

If a hackathon form requires disclosure of pre-existing infrastructure or
technologies, disclose only what is factually used and distinguish it from the
new Federation work. A required technology field is not an authorship credit.

Current integration names that may require factual technical disclosure are
CharacterKit, Federation Watchtower, Google ADK/Gemini, and Google Cloud
services. Do not expand that list or describe ownership without confirmation.

## Submission actions

- Do not update, publish, submit, or resubmit Devpost materials without an
  explicit user request and confirmation of the final content.
- Treat `docs/DEVPOST_SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md` as draft prose only.
- Before any submission, compare every claim against code, tests, deployment
  evidence, and the current scope.
- If a form asks for a Codex session ID, a collaborator, a disclosure, or any
  other identity-related field, stop and ask the owner rather than guessing.
